Why video, and why AI

Built to keep up with the product

SitecoreAI changes quickly, and a traditionally filmed video would go stale quickly too. With an AI presenter, updating the script means producing a new version in minutes. There's nothing to reshoot, and it can be localized for a global audience.

The video is kept short and focused on orientation: what SitecoreAI is, why it matters to the learner, and where the training goes next. Detailed how-to content belongs in the hands-on lessons that follow.
