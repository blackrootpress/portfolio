The problem

Getting started is the hardest part

Most creative people don't lack skill. They get stuck at the start. Generic prompt lists are either too vague to act on or too narrow to fit what the person wants to make, and they give no way to make things harder once you warm up.

I wanted a tool that works like a job aid for creative practice: available whenever it's needed, quick to use, and built so the person controls their own level of challenge.

Design decisions

How it's built to help

  1. Layered challenge

    Scaffolding · desirable difficulty

    Every prompt starts as one clear, actionable task. People can then add optional layers: a constraint, a "strange turn," a seasonal lens, or an LGBTQIA+ theme. Difficulty becomes something the learner opts into, not something imposed on them.

  2. Learner choice

    Autonomy · UDL engagement

    People choose a form (fiction, poetry, creative nonfiction, visual art, photography, or mixed media) or select "Surprise me" to get one at random. Their choice of form is remembered for the next visit.

  3. Inclusive by design

    Representation · audience fit

    The LGBTQIA+ constraints are grouped into themes like queer joy, chosen family, and everyday life. They're written so identity is present without being the conflict. One general constraint says this outright: "Do not make the subject's identity the conflict."

  4. Low friction

    Performance support

    There's no account and no setup. People can save favorites on their own device and copy or share a prompt, and the space bar generates a new one. The tool takes seconds to use so the creative work gets the time.

  5. Accessible

    WCAG-minded build

    New prompts and status messages are announced to screen readers through live regions. Every control works with the keyboard and shows a visible focus state, and the layout adapts down to phone screens.

Content design

What's inside

180original prompts across 6 creative forms
42optional constraints
30"strange turn" twists
48LGBTQIA+ constraints in 8 themes

Every prompt is written to be specific enough to act on right away and open enough that two people would make very different work from it. Because each layer is written independently, any constraint or twist can be combined with any prompt.
